Business Insights & Recommendations

Key Findings from Analysis

Contract Type Impact

Month-to-month contracts have 42.7% churn rate

Compared to one-year (11.3%) and two-year (2.8%) contracts. This is the strongest predictor of churn.

Fiber Optic Challenge

Fiber optic users churn at 41.9%

Despite premium pricing, fiber customers show higher churn than DSL (19%), indicating service quality or pricing issues.

New Customer Risk

First 12 months are critical

Customers with tenure under 1 year have significantly higher churn risk, requiring focused retention efforts.

Payment Method Effect

Electronic check users are 2.5x more likely to churn

Automated payment methods (credit card, bank transfer) correlate with better retention.

Contract Optimization Strategy
